Our story

First Experience to Live

Our name is our mission: a child's first experiences — first classroom, first friends, first teachers — deserve to be fully lived, not lost behind paperwork.

Why we exist

Teachers didn't sign up for data entry

Walk into any great daycare at 4 pm and you'll see the same thing: teachers hunched over paper daily sheets and directors buried in billing spreadsheets, while the golden hours of the day slip by.

fx2live started in Brooklyn with a simple conviction: the technology that transformed every other industry can hand early educators their time back — if it's built around how a childcare center actually runs, and if it treats children's data with the seriousness it deserves.

So we built the platform we wished our own children's centers had: one system for the whole day, with an AI copilot that drafts the paperwork and a human always in charge of what gets sent.

What we believe

Our operating principles

🧒

Children first, always

Every product decision starts with one question: does this give children more present, less distracted adults? If not, we don't build it.

🤝

AI assists, humans decide

Our AI drafts, forecasts, and flags. It never sends a message, makes a judgment about a child, or replaces an educator's professional eye.

🔐

Privacy is the product

Children can't consent to how their data is used — so we hold ourselves to the strictest standard on their behalf. No selling data. No training public models on it. Ever.

🏙️

Built with real centers

We design alongside directors and teachers in Brooklyn and beyond. If a feature doesn't survive a real Monday morning drop-off, it doesn't ship.

Brooklyn born

Rooted in Flatbush
