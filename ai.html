✦ FX2 Copilot

AI that gives teachers their day back

FX2 Copilot drafts the paperwork, forecasts the week, and answers the questions — so your team can spend their energy where it matters: with children.

How it works

Assistive by design. Human by default.

FX2 Copilot never sends, decides, or diagnoses on its own. It drafts and suggests; your staff reviews and approves. Every AI-generated item is clearly labeled.

✍️

Drafts, never sends

Daily reports, messages, and incident write-ups are prepared as drafts. A staff member always reviews and approves before anything reaches a family.

🔍

Labeled & traceable

Every AI-assisted item is marked, and Copilot shows the logged data it used — so you can verify the source of every sentence.

🚫

Clear boundaries

Copilot doesn't make medical, developmental, or disciplinary judgments. It summarizes observations and leaves conclusions to qualified humans.

What Copilot does

Six assistants in one

📝

AI daily reports

Turns the day's tap-logs — meals, naps, activities, photos — into a warm, personalized narrative for each child. Teachers edit and approve in one screen. What took an hour takes minutes.

📅

Smart staffing forecasts

Learns your center's attendance patterns to predict tomorrow's headcount by room, then proposes schedules that hold ratios with the fewest gaps — before problems happen.

📈

Enrollment intelligence

Projects openings as children age up, models waitlist conversion, and flags rooms trending toward under-enrollment months in advance so you can market ahead of the gap.

🩹

Incident report assistant

A teacher types two sentences; Copilot drafts a clear, objective, licensing-appropriate incident report with the required fields, notification checklist, and signature routing.

💬

Ask FX2

A natural-language assistant over your own center's data. "Who hasn't submitted immunization updates?" "Draft a newsletter about water play week." Ask; review; done.

🖼️

Smart photo organization

With per-child, opt-in consent, Copilot sorts classroom photos to each child's private feed and flags any photo containing a child whose family opted out of sharing.

✦ Spotlight

From tap-logs to a story parents actually read

Parents don't want a data table — they want to know their child had a good day. Copilot writes the story; your teachers make it true.

  • Grounded in logged data: every sentence traces back to an entry a teacher made.
  • Teacher voice controls: playful, warm, or concise — pick the tone per classroom.
  • Milestone-aware: highlights firsts and developmental moments worth celebrating.
  • Multilingual: reports translate into each family's preferred language automatically.

Responsible AI

Children's data deserves a higher standard

We built FX2 Copilot for the most sensitive customer data there is. These commitments are contractual, not marketing.

🔐

Your data is never used to train public models

Center and child data is used only to provide the service to you. We don't sell it, and we don't use it to train models shared with anyone else.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent consent controls

Families control photo sharing and AI-assisted features for their child. Opt-outs are enforced across the platform automatically.

🗑️

Deletion that means deletion

When a family leaves and requests deletion, their child's data is removed from active systems and purged from backups on a fixed schedule.

🧾

Full audit trail

Every AI draft, edit, and approval is logged — who reviewed it, what changed, and when it was sent. Licensing visits become boring, in the best way.
