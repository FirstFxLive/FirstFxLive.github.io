Before you write

Quick answers

How long does onboarding take?

Most single-site centers are fully live within two weeks of signing up, including data migration and staff training. Multi-site groups get a tailored rollout plan.

Can you import data from our current software?

Yes — we migrate from all major childcare platforms and from spreadsheets, free of charge on every plan.

Do parents have to pay for the app?

Never. Parent accounts and the family app are free and unlimited on every plan.

Is fx2live available outside the US?

Our platform is currently built for US-based programs (billing, subsidy, and licensing workflows are US-focused). If you're elsewhere, write to us anyway — we'd love to hear what you need.
