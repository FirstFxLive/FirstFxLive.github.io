One platform for the whole day

Check-in & attendance

A calm, contactless morning rush

Families check in from a lobby kiosk, a QR code, or their own phone. Every arrival, departure, and transfer is time-stamped, signed, and audit-ready.

  • Kiosk, QR & mobile check-in with PIN or digital signature.
  • Authorized pickup verification with photo ID matching and instant alerts for unlisted adults.
  • Live classroom rosters so every teacher knows exactly who is in the room.
  • Absence reporting by parents right from the app — no more morning phone calls.
  • Licensing-ready attendance exports for state audits and subsidy claims.

Billing & payments

Tuition that collects itself

Set your rates once. fx2live invoices every family on schedule, runs autopay, chases late payments politely, and reconciles every penny.

  • Recurring autopay via card or bank transfer, with family-managed payment methods.
  • Flexible plans: weekly, monthly, drop-in, sibling discounts, registration and supply fees.
  • Subsidy & agency billing with co-pay splits and third-party statements.
  • Automatic late fees and reminders so you never have an awkward conversation again.
  • Year-end tax statements generated for every family in one click.
  • Revenue dashboard with aging, forecasts, and per-program breakdowns.
