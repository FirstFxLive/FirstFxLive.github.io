✦ FX2 Copilot

The first AI copilot built for early education

Most daycare software digitizes paperwork. fx2live eliminates it. FX2 Copilot works alongside your team all day — drafting, forecasting, and flagging — always with a human in the loop.

  • AI daily reports: turns quick taps into warm, personalized summaries parents love.
  • Smart staffing forecasts: predicts attendance and suggests schedules that keep ratios compliant.
  • Enrollment intelligence: projects openings months ahead and prioritizes your waitlist automatically.
  • Incident report assistant: drafts clear, objective incident documentation from a teacher's short note.
